Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We’ve Got You Covered.

Find quick answers about custom tailoring, uniforms, measurements, bulk orders, and placing an order with GERS STYLES.

Do you make clothes to individual measurements?

Yes. GERS STYLES offers custom tailoring based on your measurements, preferred fit, style, and intended occasion.

What types of clothing do you make?

We create traditional wear, suits and formal wear, school uniforms, corporate and work uniforms, medical scrubs, sweaters, and other custom-made garments.

Can schools and companies place bulk uniform orders?

Yes. We accept individual and bulk orders for schools, businesses, organizations, medical facilities, and other institutions.

How long does an order take?

Turnaround time depends on the garment, quantity, customization, and current workload. We confirm the expected completion time when discussing your order.

Can I request a specific design or style?

Yes. You can share your preferred design, reference image, colours, fabric requirements, or ideas, and we’ll discuss how best to create the garment for you.

Do you provide fabric, or can I bring my own?

Customers can discuss fabric requirements with us before production. If you already have suitable fabric, contact us and we’ll advise whether it works for your chosen design.

How do I place an order?

Send an inquiry through our website or contact GERS STYLES directly by phone, WhatsApp, or email. We’ll discuss your requirements, measurements, quantity, and timeline.

Where is GERS STYLES located?

We serve customers through Kampala and Fort Portal, Uganda. Contact us before visiting so we can guide you appropriately.
